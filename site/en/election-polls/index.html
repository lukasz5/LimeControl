LimeSurvey · Poland module

Election polls in Poland

How did support change between two survey waves? A research agency measures voting intentions in LimeSurvey. The committee can see the results for its area alongside campaign dates and expenditure.

Download plugin ↓ Ask about a paid pilot

The plugin interface and example surveys are in Polish.

From study design to the committee report

  1. Define the population

    Select the area, election and ballot. Agree on the target population, recruitment method and fieldwork dates. Official results from the same election year provide local context.

  2. Run two survey waves

    Link two anonymous surveys with matching questions on voting intention and area. Check the counts of completed interviews and decided respondents in both waves.

  3. Keep a campaign calendar

    The coordinator records events and related tasks, assigns owners and deadlines, and enters planned and actual costs. Planner access can be granted separately.

  4. Review the results together

    Compare up to four non-overlapping areas. The committee can receive access to an aggregate report. The plugin suppresses results when response counts are too small.

What did the committee spend, and how did support change?

Each county recorded 40 completed interviews per wave. In each group, 25 respondents chose a list. They form the denominator for the share of List A.

Across both counties, support rose from 40% to 60%. The county figures reveal that the changes ran in opposite directions. Event dates show what happened during fieldwork. Assessing the effect of a particular activity requires a separate study.

Which official Polish results are included?

2018 · 2024Regional assemblies
Party-list vote totals from national level down to polling district, with ballots for individual constituencies.
2014 · 2018 · 2024Local councils and mayors
Municipal, city and county councils and Warsaw district councils. Mayoral results cover every round held in each municipality.

The archive works offline after installation. For 2014 the finest available level is the municipality or constituency. Check boundaries and list composition before comparing election years.

Election day exit polling

A research agency can set up a separate study for a particular ballot and round.

  • Draw polling districts within strata and set the interval for inviting voters as they leave. The interval can vary with district size.
  • Record anonymous ballots and refusals by shift. Read an interval that accounts for district sampling and check how different preferences among people who declined would change the estimate.
  • Once a verified official tally is available, use it in place of the district estimate in the late poll. The original exit poll remains available for comparison.

Set up the project in LimeSurvey

Upload the ZIP in the plugin manager and activate it. Import the two LSS files as separate surveys. Open Sondaże wyborcze (Election polls) and link them as two waves of one project.
