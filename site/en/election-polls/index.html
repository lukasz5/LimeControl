LimeControl Elections

Run your committee’s campaign from one calendar. For each event, see who is responsible, what is due and how actual spending compares with the budget.

Create a campaign on your LimeSurvey installation to start scheduling events, budgeting and planning a daily itinerary. You can link two survey waves to the same campaign later.

Poland

See how a committee prepares for a debate and shares the approved route with selected team members. They sign in on their phones, open directions and mark each stop as visited.

From planning an event to checking the bill

Plan the calendar

Enter a meeting or debate, set the dates and select the area. Open the day’s route map to check distances between stops and estimated driving times before the next meeting.

Assign the work

Link a task such as preparing materials to the event, name the person or team responsible and set a deadline. Mark the task complete when it is done.

Track spending

Enter the budget for the event and any tasks with a cost. Record actual amounts as they come in. The summary counts each item once and flags missing amounts.
