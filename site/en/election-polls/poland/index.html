What did the debate cost?

The event and its linked task each have a budget. After the debate, the coordinator marks the event as held and the team records the cost of materials. Each expense appears once in the total.

Can the team collect the leaflets before the debate?

The team leaves the office at 9:00. Collecting the leaflets from the printer takes 15 minutes, and the library debate starts at 10:30. Enter the stops and times. The map shows the full route, with a distance and estimated driving time for each leg. Review the suggested order and arrival time before saving the change. Approve the route, select the accounts of the people going to the debate and send them the link. Once signed in on their phones, they can open directions to the print shop. Marking it as visited updates the team’s shared progress.

The offline estimate uses 1.30 times the straight-line distance and an average speed of 45 km/h. With an OSRM server configured, you can calculate the journey along roads. Both options show waiting time, time at stops and any late arrivals.

What did the committee spend, and how did support change?

After both waves, the committee can compare campaign activities and changes in voting intention across up to four non-overlapping areas. In this example each county recorded 40 completed interviews per wave. In each group, 25 respondents chose a list. They form the denominator for the share of List A.

The archive works offline after installation. For 2014 the finest available level is the municipality or constituency. Check boundaries and list composition before comparing election years.

Election day exit polling

A research agency can set up a separate study for a particular ballot and round.

  • Draw polling districts within strata and set the interval for inviting voters as they leave. The interval can vary with district size.
  • Record anonymous ballots and refusals by shift. Read an interval that accounts for district sampling and check how different preferences among people who declined would change the estimate.
  • Once a verified official tally is available, use it in place of the district estimate in the late poll. The original exit poll remains available for comparison.

Set up the planner in LimeSurvey

Install and activate the ZIP in the plugin manager. Open Planery kampanii (Campaign planners) and create a campaign. Schedule an event, assign tasks and plan your stops. To practise linking waves later, import and activate the two example surveys and attach them to the same campaign.
