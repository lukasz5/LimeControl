Privacy

LimeControl privacy policy

Version 1.1 · effective from 19 November 2026

LimeControl is a tool for managing your LimeSurvey server. Survey data and respondents’ answers stay on that server — we neither collect nor store them.

  • We show no ads and do not sell data. We collect visit statistics for the limecontrol.eu website (Google Analytics) only with your consent; the apps contain no tracking tools.
  • LimeSurvey passwords are not stored in our database or in browser storage.
  • The mobile app works without an account — server profiles are saved on the device only.
  • If you run LimeControl on your own server, we receive no data at all.

Controller and contact

The controller of personal data processed in connection with the limecontrol.eu website, the app.limecontrol.eu service and the LimeControl mobile app is Strefabadań.pl – Łukasz Szemiako, Wały Jagiellońskie 28/30 lok. 2, 80-853 Gdańsk, Poland, tax ID (NIP) 9571090805.

Contact for privacy matters: info@strefabadan.pl.

The limecontrol.eu website

  • Server logs. The server records the IP address, the time of the request, the page address and the browser identifier. They serve security and error diagnosis (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Cookies and statistics. Google Analytics cookies are set only with your consent — see Cookies and statistics.
  • Fonts. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives the browser’s IP address.
  • Downloads. You download the themes, survey templates and plugins without registering.
  • Contact. When you write to us, we process the message and your contact details in order to reply.

Cookies and statistics

With your consent, the limecontrol.eu website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits, the popularity of pages and downloads, and traffic sources. Without consent the Google Analytics script is not loaded and no cookies are set.

  • Legal basis. Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the national rules implementing Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive). You can withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.
  • Scope. A browser identifier stored in a cookie, the pages visited, the referring page, approximate location (country, city), and the device and browser type. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off — the data is not used for advertising.
  • Recipient. Google Ireland Limited. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA under the European Commission’s adequacy decision on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Retention. Statistics data is deleted after 14 months.

The legal basis is the provision of the service you use (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure (point (f)).

In direct mode the browser connects to your LimeSurvey server without us in between — the user name, the password and the survey data never reach our servers.

Mobile app

  • No account. The app for iOS and Android requires no registration or sign-in with us.
  • Data on the device. Server profiles (name, address, user name, settings) and preferences are saved only in the app’s storage on your device. They disappear when you uninstall it.
  • Passwords. The LimeSurvey password is not stored; you can only keep it until you close the app.
  • Connections. The app connects directly to the LimeSurvey server you specify. If you connect it to your own LimeControl server, the access key for it is kept on the device.
  • No tracking. The app contains no ads, analytics tools or tracking libraries and sends us no usage data.
  • Permissions. The app does not ask for access to location, contacts, the camera or the microphone. You save or share exported files through the system share sheet.

Self-hosted installation

LimeControl running on your own server (the access-key mode) does not connect to our infrastructure. Profiles are saved in the browser and the scheduler queue on your server. You or your organisation are the controller of that data.

Survey and respondent data

Surveys, participants and responses are stored in your LimeSurvey. LimeControl displays them and lets you manage them, but does not collect them. The controller of respondents’ data is the organisation running the study — it is responsible for the legal basis and for informing participants.

Exported responses are saved on your device. From that moment you decide how they are stored.

The StrefaAnkiet platform, to which you can link a survey, has its own privacy policy.

Providers

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Fonts, Google Analytics) — sign-in, profile storage in the app.limecontrol.eu service, the website fonts and — with your consent — website visit statistics. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area on the basis of standard contractual clauses.
  • Hosting provider — the servers that run the website and the app.

Retention

  • The account and profiles in the app.limecontrol.eu service — until you delete them or request deletion of the account.
  • The password in a scheduled job — until the job has run or been cancelled; the job history — until you delete it in the app.
  • Google Analytics statistics — 14 months; your consent choice — 12 months in your browser’s storage.
  • Server logs — for the limited time needed to keep the service secure and diagnose errors.
  • Correspondence — for as long as needed to handle the matter and for the limitation period of claims.

Deleting your account. You can delete profiles yourself on the Instances tab. To delete the account together with all its data, write to info@strefabadan.pl from the address tied to the account.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict its processing and to data portability, as well as the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Send your request to info@strefabadan.pl. You may also lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in Poland or with your local supervisory authority.

Providing data is voluntary, but signing in with a Google account is necessary to use the app.limecontrol.eu service. We make no automated decisions and do not profile users.

Changes to this policy

We will announce material changes on this page with a new effective date. Earlier versions are available on request.
