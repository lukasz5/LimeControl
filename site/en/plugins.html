Free plugins for LimeSurvey

Plugins for LimeSurvey. Free and open source.

Two groups to choose from. Open plugins run in your LimeSurvey with no account and no sign-up. StrefaAnkiet plugins connect the survey to the platform’s services: managed mailing through Amazon SES, VoIP telephony for CATI interviews, interviewers in the field and analysis of the results. The code of both groups is free — you pay only for the StrefaAnkiet account and the services you use.

Open plugins No account

Open plugins for your own LimeSurvey installation

You install them in your LimeSurvey and use them without signing up and with no time limit. Each one installs separately — only the ones you need. Dependent JSON questions, the CRM and shop customer feedback are separate LimeControl plugins with their own download packages.

A campaign planner for election committees. Schedule events, assign tasks and compare actual spending with the budget. The Poland module also shows campaign activities alongside two waves of polling.

LimeSurvey plugin

Questionnaire exchange

StrefaSurveyExchange

Downloads a questionnaire from LimeSurvey as an .lss structure or a JSON package with a field map and a difference report.

  • Export of the structure (.lss) or a JSON package with a field-code map
  • A list of elements that cannot be transferred — nothing is silently simplified
  • Settings of other plugins are stripped from the export

Runs in LimeSurvey (tested on 6.15 and 7.10)

LimeSurvey plugin

Response export

StrefaResponseExport

Provides coded responses of an active survey for analysis — paginated and without technical identifiers.

  • Skips tokens, IP addresses and the referrer
  • Requires read and export permissions for the specific survey
  • Download of attachments from file-upload questions

Runs in LimeSurvey (tested on 6.15 and 7.10)

Command-line tool

LSS, JSON and CSV converters

StrefaConverters

Standalone tools for converting questionnaires and responses — useful for migrations and archiving.

  • LSS ↔ JSON with a SHA-256 checksum
  • Responses from JSON, CSV and the VV export
  • Keeps codes with leading zeros (e.g. 001) as text

Runs in The command line, outside LimeSurvey

LimeSurvey plugin

Dependent JSON questions

LimeControlJsonQuestions

Build follow-up questions from earlier answers. After a respondent chooses cities and activities, ask how many hours they spent on each pair and request an explanation above a threshold.

  • Three question themes for per-item choices, tables and follow-ups
  • A visual editor for visibility, required answers and comment rules
  • Each block uses one response field, including large matrices; long-format CSV export with a codebook

Runs in LimeSurvey CE (tested on 6.15.12, 6.17.19, 7.11.0)

CRM: companies, contacts and cases

LimeControlCrm

Adds a CRM item to the LimeSurvey admin panel: companies, contacts, sales opportunities, tasks and customer cases. Once the inquiry form is submitted, a request to be contacted reaches the assigned team as a sales opportunity or a service case.

  • Teams built on LimeSurvey user groups: the manager sees the team, staff see their own records
  • A sales pipeline, change history and a report of amounts per currency
  • Contact import from CSV with a duplicate preview before saving

LimeSurvey plugin

Customer feedback for online shops

LimeControlFeedback

Sends surveys after a purchase, after delivery, after a period of product use and after a return — based on the shop’s orders. The report shows CSAT, NPS and variant ratings next to return reasons. It works on its own or together with the CRM.

  • WooCommerce, PrestaShop, OpenCart, Shopify and Shoper connectors with signed order events
  • One invitation per order and campaign, a minimum gap between campaigns and a confirmed opt-out
  • If the customer agrees to be contacted, a return request or a low rating in a named survey opens a service case — in the plugin itself or in the CRM

Runs in LimeSurvey CE (tested on 6.15.12, 7.11.0) and your shop

Version 0.1.0

StrefaAnkiet plugins StrefaAnkiet account

They connect LimeSurvey to StrefaAnkiet services

The plugins are free and open source, but they need a StrefaAnkiet account to work. Respondents still complete the same LimeSurvey survey — the platform adds mailing, interview fieldwork and analysis.

LimeSurvey plugin

Study menu

StrefaWorkspace

Links a LimeSurvey survey to a study in StrefaAnkiet and adds shortcuts to the whole workspace to the survey menu.

  • CAWI, CATI and CAPI fieldwork, results, analyses, respondents and team — from within the survey
  • An explicit mapping of the survey ID (SID) to a study
  • Links grant no permissions — the platform roles still apply

Connects to The team workspace in StrefaAnkiet

LimeSurvey plugin

Mailing through Amazon SES

StrefaInvitationRelay

LimeSurvey invitations and reminders sent through your company’s Amazon SES channel in StrefaAnkiet — without changing how you work with participants.

  • Recipients, tokens and content stay in LimeSurvey
  • Delivery confirmations, bounces and complaints in the mailing history; protection against duplicate sending
  • AWS keys stay on the platform side

LimeSurvey plugin

CATI and CAPI fieldwork, analysis

StrefaResearch

A research workspace embedded in LimeSurvey: interviewer assignments, telephone fieldwork coordination and response analysis.

  • CAPI: assignments, targets and deadlines for interviewers; the interview runs in the native LimeSurvey survey
  • CATI: sample, case queue, assignments, callbacks and sample quotas in one view
  • Analysis: dataset versions, variable dictionary, analyses in R, reports

Connects to VoIP telephony (CATI), fieldwork (CAPI), analysis

StrefaAnkiet suite. The suite is at release-candidate stage 0.9 (pilot), tested on LimeSurvey 6.15 and 7.10. How it works

Free software, paid services

You install the software on your own server for free. What is paid is what cannot be handed out as code: mailing infrastructure, telephony and a team account on the platform.

Free of charge

Plugins and tools

You install them in your LimeSurvey and use them with no time limit.

  • Six StrefaAnkiet plugins and tools plus four LimeControl plugins for LimeSurvey — with source code
  • Questionnaire exchange, response export and the converters work without an account
  • The LimeControl app (iOS, Android, web), themes and survey templates
  • Docker deployment kits for LimeSurvey and LimeControl

StrefaAnkiet account

Platform services

A platform subscription plus add-on services billed by usage. You match the scope to your study.

  • Managed Amazon SES — you enter the sender and add three DNS records; no AWS account of your own and no keys
  • VoIP telephony for CATI — a phone in the interviewer’s browser, the StrefaAnkiet VoIP server, a number, calls and recordings
  • Fieldwork (CAPI) — interviewer assignments and targets, an interviewer app that works offline
  • Team workspace — projects, roles, quotas across modes, analyses in R and PDF/DOCX reports

Telephony, bulk mailing, transcription and AI need separate configuration and have their own billing terms.

Connecting with LimeControl

Fieldwork progress in the app

If a survey is linked to a study in StrefaAnkiet, LimeControl shows its fieldwork status — without opening the panel.

How to connect your account
  • CAPI — completed interviews, interviewer assignments and their progress against the target.
  • CATI — the number of cases in the queue, unassigned cases and cases that need attention.
  • Read-only — the app changes nothing in the study; it needs an access key with the fieldwork.read and cati.read scopes.
  • The key stays with you — it is kept only in the memory of the open tab, just like LimeSurvey passwords.
Getting started

Four steps — the first three are free

  1. Set up a survey server

    Use your own LimeSurvey instance, the free Docker kit, or let us handle deployment and maintenance.

  2. Add a theme and a template

    Install the CATI or CAPI theme and import an interview skeleton.

  3. Install the free plugins

    Upload the packages in the LimeSurvey plugin manager and state which survey belongs to which study.

  4. Switch services on when you need them

    Create a StrefaAnkiet account, set up mailing or telephony and follow the progress in LimeControl.

Planning a CATI or CAPI study?

Describe the study — the number of interviews, the modes and the dates. We will tell you what the free tools can do and where a StrefaAnkiet account helps.
