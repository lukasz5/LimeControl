Free plugins for LimeSurvey

Connect LimeSurvey with StrefaAnkiet. The plugins are free.

The StrefaAnkiet plugins for LimeSurvey are free of charge and open source. They connect your survey to the StrefaAnkiet platform — and you pay only for the account and the services you use: managed mailing through Amazon SES, VoIP telephony for CATI interviews, interviewers in the field and analysis of the results. Respondents still complete the same LimeSurvey survey.

How it works

Free software, paid services

You install the software on your own server for free. What is paid is what cannot be handed out as code: mailing infrastructure, telephony and a team account on the platform.

Platform services

A platform subscription plus add-on services billed by usage. You match the scope to your study.

  • Managed Amazon SES — you enter the sender and add three DNS records; no AWS account of your own and no keys
  • VoIP telephony for CATI — a phone in the interviewer’s browser, the StrefaAnkiet VoIP server, a number, calls and recordings
  • Fieldwork (CAPI) — interviewer assignments and targets, an interviewer app that works offline
  • Team workspace — projects, roles, quotas across modes, analyses in R and PDF/DOCX reports

Telephony, bulk mailing, transcription and AI need separate configuration and have their own billing terms.

The plugin suite

StrefaAnkiet for LimeSurvey

Six independent components — you install only the ones you need. The plugins do not add their own tables to the LimeSurvey database.

Needs a StrefaAnkiet account

Study menu

Plugin: StrefaWorkspace

Links a LimeSurvey survey to a study in StrefaAnkiet and adds shortcuts to the whole workspace to the survey menu.

  • CAWI, CATI and CAPI fieldwork, results, analyses, respondents and team — from within the survey
  • An explicit mapping of the survey ID (SID) to a study
  • Links grant no permissions — the platform roles still apply
Needs a StrefaAnkiet account

Mailing through Amazon SES

Plugin: StrefaInvitationRelay

LimeSurvey invitations and reminders sent through your company’s Amazon SES channel in StrefaAnkiet — without changing how you work with participants.

  • Recipients, tokens and content stay in LimeSurvey
  • Delivery confirmations, bounces and complaints in the mailing history
  • Protection against duplicate sending
  • AWS keys stay on the platform side
Needs a StrefaAnkiet account

CATI and CAPI fieldwork, analysis

Plugin: StrefaResearch

A research workspace embedded in LimeSurvey: interviewer assignments, telephone fieldwork coordination and response analysis.

  • CAPI: assignments, targets and deadlines for interviewers; the interview runs in the native LimeSurvey survey
  • CATI: sample, case queue, assignments, callbacks and handovers between interviewers
  • Sample quotas and fieldwork progress in one view
  • Analysis: dataset versions, variable dictionary, analyses in R, reports
Works without an account

Questionnaire exchange

Plugin: StrefaSurveyExchange

Downloads a questionnaire from LimeSurvey as an .lss structure or a JSON package with a field map and a difference report.

  • Export of the structure (.lss) or a JSON package with a field-code map
  • A list of elements that cannot be transferred — nothing is silently simplified
  • Settings of other plugins are stripped from the export
Works without an account

Response export

Plugin: StrefaResponseExport

Provides coded responses of an active survey for analysis — paginated and without technical identifiers.

  • Skips tokens, IP addresses and the referrer
  • Requires read and export permissions for the specific survey
  • Download of attachments from file-upload questions
Works without an account

Converters (command line)

Tool package: StrefaConverters

Standalone tools for converting questionnaires and responses — useful for migrations and archiving.

  • LSS ↔ JSON with a SHA-256 checksum
  • Responses from JSON, CSV and the VV export
  • Keeps codes with leading zeros (e.g. 001) as text

Release status. The suite is at release-candidate stage 0.9 (pilot), tested on LimeSurvey 6.15 and 7.10. A public repository with the installation packages is being prepared — until then write to us and we will send you the plugins free of charge together with the guide.

Connecting with LimeControl

Fieldwork progress in the app

If a survey is linked to a study in StrefaAnkiet, LimeControl shows its fieldwork status — without opening the panel.

How to connect your account
  • CAPI — completed interviews, interviewer assignments and their progress against the target.
  • CATI — the number of cases in the queue, unassigned cases and cases that need attention.
  • Read-only — the app changes nothing in the study; it needs an access key with the fieldwork.read and cati.read scopes.
  • The key stays with you — it is kept only in the memory of the open tab, just like LimeSurvey passwords.
Getting started

Four steps — the first three are free

  1. Set up a survey server

    Use your own LimeSurvey instance, the free Docker kit, or let us handle deployment and maintenance.

  2. Add a theme and a template

    Install the CATI or CAPI theme and import an interview skeleton.

  3. Install the free plugins

    Upload the packages in the LimeSurvey plugin manager and state which survey belongs to which study.

  4. Switch services on when you need them

    Create a StrefaAnkiet account, set up mailing or telephony and follow the progress in LimeControl.
