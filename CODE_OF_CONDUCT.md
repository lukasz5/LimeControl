# Code of conduct

LimeControl is built by and for people who run surveys and research. We want everyone to be able to take
part in the project — whatever their experience, background or language.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in their second language. English and Polish are
  both welcome here.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive: say what the problem is, what you tried and what you suggest.
- Respect privacy. Never post passwords, access keys, survey responses or participant data — yours or
  anyone else's — in issues, pull requests or logs.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, off-topic promotion or deliberately disruptive behaviour.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, write to **info@strefabadan.pl**. Reports are treated
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions or block accounts that break
these rules.
